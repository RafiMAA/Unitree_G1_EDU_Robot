# PickMe — Sri Lanka's Leading Ride-Hailing Service

## What is PickMe?

PickMe is Sri Lanka's number one ride-hailing platform, connecting passengers with verified drivers across the country. Think of it as Sri Lanka's version of Uber — but built specifically for Sri Lankan roads, culture, and needs.

### Official App Logo

The official PickMe app logo has a **yellow background with a black passenger figure**. It is not green. Passengers should use this appearance to identify the official app in the App Store or Google Play Store.

## Why Install PickMe?

- **Safe and Verified**: Every PickMe driver undergoes background checks and vehicle inspections
- **Fair Pricing**: Metered fares regulated by the government — no surge pricing surprises
- **Multiple Vehicle Types**: From budget tuk-tuks to luxury sedans
- **Cashless or Cash**: Pay with card, PickMe wallet, or cash — your choice
- **24/7 Availability**: Rides available around the clock, across all of Sri Lanka
- **Trip Tracking**: Share your live location with family and friends for safety
- **In-App Support**: Dedicated customer support in English, Sinhala, and Tamil

## How PickMe Works

1. **Open the App** — Launch PickMe on your phone
2. **Set Destination** — Type or speak your destination
3. **Choose Vehicle** — Select from Mini, Car, Van, or Flash
4. **Confirm Booking** — See the estimated fare and confirm
5. **Track Driver** — Watch your driver approach on the live map
6. **Ride** — The driver arrives, you get in, enjoy the ride
7. **Pay** — Fare is calculated automatically. Pay via app or cash
8. **Rate** — Rate your experience to help maintain quality

## Payment Methods

- **Cash**: Pay the driver directly at the end of the ride
- **Credit/Debit Card**: Visa, Mastercard linked to the app
- **PickMe Wallet**: Prepaid digital wallet — top up and ride
- **Corporate Accounts**: For business travelers

## Safety Features

- **GPS Tracking**: Every trip is GPS-tracked and logged
- **Share Trip**: Send your live trip status to emergency contacts
- **Emergency Button**: In-app SOS button connects to emergency services
- **Trip Insurance**: Passenger insurance included on all rides

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need a Sri Lankan phone number?**
A: Yes, you'll need a local SIM card or a number that can receive SMS for OTP verification. SIM cards are available at the airport for around Rs. 300-500.

**Q: Can I pay in foreign currency?**
A: PickMe fares are in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR). International credit/debit cards are accepted. You can also use cash (LKR).

**Q: Is PickMe available outside Colombo?**
A: Yes! PickMe operates across Sri Lanka — Colombo, Kandy, Galle, Negombo, Jaffna, and many more cities.

**Q: How is the fare calculated?**
A: Fares are based on distance traveled and time taken. You'll see an estimate before confirming your ride.

**Q: What if my driver cancels?**
A: PickMe will automatically search for another driver nearby. You won't be charged for cancellations by the driver.

**Q: Is PickMe safe for solo travelers?**
A: Absolutely. PickMe is Sri Lanka's most trusted ride platform. Use the trip-sharing feature and emergency button for extra peace of mind.
