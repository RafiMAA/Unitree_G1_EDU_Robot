# Sri Lanka — Key Locations for Travelers

## Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA)

- **Location**: Katunayake, about 35 km north of Colombo city center
- **IATA Code**: CMB
- **Travel Time to Colombo**: 45 minutes to 1.5 hours depending on traffic
- **PickMe Pickup Point**: Exit the arrivals hall, proceed to the designated ride-hailing pickup lane on the right side

## Useful Tips for Travelers

- **Currency**: Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). 1 USD ≈ 300 LKR (approximate)
- **SIM Cards**: Available at the airport arrivals hall. Dialog, Mobitel, and Airtel are popular providers
- **Traffic**: Colombo traffic is heavy during rush hours (7-9 AM, 5-7 PM). Plan accordingly
- **Tipping**: Not mandatory but appreciated. Round up or add 10% for good service
- **Emergency Number**: 119 (Police), 110 (Ambulance)
