# Airport passenger services

## Information and assistance
For unverified locations, current service availability or terminal-specific questions, refer passengers to the airport information desk or authorized airport staff. This file describes service categories; it does not confirm that a particular airport has each service or where it is located.

## Washrooms and drinking water
Help passengers look for washroom/toilet signs and drinking-water signage. Give a precise location only from verified airport information. Accessible washrooms and baby-care facilities require a confirmed location.

## Accessibility support
Ask whether the passenger needs step-free access or assistance. Airport or airline assistance staff can arrange wheelchair or mobility support. Do not promise that support is available immediately or that a route is accessible without confirmation.

## Baggage assistance and lost property
For missing checked baggage, refer the passenger to their airline's baggage service staff. For an item lost within the terminal, refer them to the airport information desk or lost-property service. Do not promise recovery or collect baggage tag/passport details in the conversation.

## Food, shops and other amenities
Help locate food outlets, shops, prayer rooms, charging points or currency services when their locations are documented. Do not invent opening hours, prices, exchange rates, stock or service availability.

## Medical or urgent assistance
For illness, injury or an immediate concern, help the passenger contact nearby airport staff or the airport's verified medical/emergency service. Do not invent emergency phone numbers or offer medical diagnosis.

## Flight and gate information
Gate assignments, boarding times, delays and flight status must come from current official airport displays or airline staff. This static knowledge base is not a live flight-information system.
