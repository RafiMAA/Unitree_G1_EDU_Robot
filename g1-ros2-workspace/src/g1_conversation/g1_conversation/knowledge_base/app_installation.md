# Installing the PickMe App — Step-by-Step Guide

## Download the App

### For iPhone (iOS)
1. Open the **App Store** on your iPhone
2. Search for **"PickMe"**
3. Look for the official PickMe app: a yellow logo with a black passenger figure
4. Tap **"Get"** or the download button
5. Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or Apple ID password
6. Wait for the app to download and install

### For Android
1. Open **Google Play Store** on your phone
2. Search for **"PickMe"**
3. Look for the official PickMe app: a yellow logo with a black passenger figure
4. Tap **"Install"**
5. Wait for the app to download and install

### QR Code
If you're having trouble finding the app, you can scan a QR code that links directly to the PickMe app in your app store. The robot can display this QR code for you.

## Registration Process

### Step 1: Open the App
Launch PickMe after installation. You'll see a welcome screen.

### Step 2: Enter Your Phone Number
- Enter your phone number with country code
- You'll need a number that can receive SMS messages
- A Sri Lankan SIM card works best (available at the airport for Rs. 300-500)
- International numbers are also supported

### Step 3: Verify with OTP
- PickMe will send a One-Time Password (OTP) via SMS
- Enter the 4-6 digit code in the app
- If you don't receive it within 60 seconds, tap "Resend OTP"

### Step 4: Set Up Your Profile
- Enter your name
- Add an email address (optional but recommended)
- Choose your preferred language

### Step 5: Grant Permissions
The app will ask for several permissions. Here's why each one matters:

| Permission | Why It's Needed |
|---|---|
| **Location** | To find your current position and nearby drivers |
| **Notifications** | To alert you when your driver is arriving |
| **Phone** | To call your driver directly from the app |

All permissions are for your convenience and safety. Your data is protected by PickMe's privacy policy.

### Step 6: Add Payment Method (Optional)
- You can skip this and pay with cash
- To add a card: Go to Payment → Add Card → Enter card details
- Visa and Mastercard are accepted
- You can also top up the PickMe Wallet

## You're Ready!
Once registration is complete, you can book your first ride immediately. Just:
1. Enter your destination
2. Choose your vehicle type
3. Confirm and wait for your driver

## Troubleshooting

**"OTP not received"**
- Check your phone has signal
- Make sure your SIM card is activated
- Try the "Resend OTP" option
- Check if SMS is being blocked by any filters

**"Location not found"**
- Make sure location/GPS is enabled on your phone
- Move to an area with better signal
- Manually enter your pickup location

**"Payment declined"**
- Check card details are entered correctly
- Ensure your card supports international transactions
- Try a different card or switch to cash payment
